Electronics Tinkering

I love taking things apart to understand how they work. From vintage radios to modern IoT devices, reverse engineering hardware teaches me invaluable lessons about design decisions and engineering trade-offs.

Notable Projects & Accomplishments

  • Modified vintage radio to add Bluetooth connectivity
  • Reverse engineered a smart home device protocol
  • Built custom Arduino-based automation systems
  • Repaired and upgraded retro gaming consoles

Mechanical Keyboards

Mechanical keyboards combine engineering, aesthetics, and functionality. I design custom PCBs, hand-solder switches, and program firmware to create unique typing experiences.

Notable Projects & Accomplishments

  • Designed and built a 60% split keyboard
  • Created custom QMK firmware with advanced macros
  • 3D printed custom keyboard cases
  • Experimented with different switch types and keycap profiles
🖨️

3D Printing

3D printing bridges the gap between digital design and physical reality. I use it to create custom enclosures for electronics projects, replacement parts, and functional prototypes.

Notable Projects & Accomplishments

  • Designed enclosures for Raspberry Pi projects
  • Created custom mounting brackets for sensors
  • Printed replacement parts for vintage electronics
  • Experimented with different materials (PLA, PETG, TPU)
🎮

Retro Gaming

Classic game consoles are fascinating pieces of engineering history. I collect, restore, and sometimes modify them to keep these pieces of computing history alive.

Notable Projects & Accomplishments

  • Restored original Nintendo Entertainment System
  • Built a MAME arcade cabinet from scratch
  • Modified Game Boy for backlit screen
  • Developed FPGA-based console emulators
